# User Journey Maps for Major E‑Commerce Platforms

Below are high‑level, page‑by‑page user journeys for a selection of **large e‑commerce giants** and **popular open‑source/ SaaS platforms**. The flows capture the typical steps a shopper experiences, from landing on the home page through product discovery, checkout, order confirmation, and post‑order actions such as tracking, returns, or cancellations.

---

## 1. Amazon (global marketplace)
1. **Home Page** – Personalized banners, deals, search bar.
2. **Search / Category Browse** – Results with filters & sorting.
3. **Product Detail Page** – Images, variations, reviews, "Add to Cart".
4. **Add‑to‑Cart Confirmation** – Mini‑cart overlay.
5. **Cart Page** – Review items, update qty, apply coupons.
6. **Proceed to Checkout** – Sign‑in / create account (or guest with email OTP).
7. **Shipping Address** – Select or add new address, Amazon‑Lock‑box options.
8. **Delivery Options** – Choose standard, Prime, same‑day, lockers.
9. **Payment** – Saved cards, Amazon Pay, gift cards, promotional codes.
10. **Review Order** – Final confirmation.
11. **Order Confirmation Page** – Order #, estimated delivery, “Track Order”.
12. **Order Tracking** – Real‑time status, map, delivery driver contact.
13. **Post‑Purchase** – “Buy Again”, “Add Review”, “Subscribe & Save”.
14. **Return / Refund** – From orders list → “Return or Replace” → select reason → schedule pickup or drop‑off → receive refund notification.
15. **Cancellation** – Cancel from orders list before shipment; confirmation email.

---

## 2. eBay (C2C & B2C marketplace)
1. **Home / Landing** – Featured listings, categories, search.
2. **Search / Category** – Filters, auction vs. Buy‑It‑Now.
3. **Product Listing** – Title, seller rating, photos, price, bidding timer.
4. **Add to Cart / Buy Now** – Option to watch or add to cart.
5. **Cart Review** – Qty, shipping estimate per seller.
6. **Sign‑In / Guest Checkout** – eBay account required for purchase.
7. **Shipping Details** – Choose address, select shipping method per seller.
8. **Payment** – PayPal, credit card, Apple Pay.
9. **Place Order** – Review, accept terms, confirm.
10. **Confirmation Page** – Order ID, seller contact, “Track Order”.
11. **Order Tracking** – Consolidated view of multiple sellers.
12. **Returns** – Initiate via “Return this item” → select reason → print label (if seller provides) → ship back.
13. **Cancellations** – Before seller ships, request cancellation; seller approves/denies.

---

## 3. Walmart (large retailer)
1. **Home** – Rotating hero carousel, grocery pickup banner, search.
2. **Search / Department** – Filters (brand, price, rating).
3. **Product Detail** – “Add to Cart”, “Add to Pickup”, “Buy Online Pickup In‑Store (BOPIS)”.
4. **Cart** – Review, apply coupons, choose delivery or pickup.
5. **Checkout** – Sign‑in / guest checkout.
6. **Shipping / Pickup Selection** – Home delivery, curbside, store pickup.
7. **Payment** – Walmart Pay, credit cards, PayPal.
8. **Review & Place Order** – Summary page.
9. **Confirmation** – Order summary, “Track Order”, “Pickup Instructions”.
10. **Order Tracking** – Delivery status or pickup slot.
11. **Return** – Initiate from order → “Return or Replace” → schedule drop‑off at store or request carrier pick‑up.
12. **Cancellation** – Cancel from orders before processing; email confirmation.

---

## 4. Alibaba / AliExpress (global B2C marketplace)
1. **Home** – Flash deals, categories, search.
2. **Search / Category** – Filters, seller ratings.
3. **Product Page** – Variants, “Add to Cart”, “Buy Now”.
4. **Cart** – Review, apply coupons.
5. **Checkout** – Sign‑in, select shipping address.
6. **Shipping Options** – Multiple carriers, estimated customs.
7. **Payment** – Alipay, credit cards, bank transfer.
8. **Place Order** – Confirmation.
9. **Confirmation** – Order ID, “Track Logistics”.
10. **Order Tracking** – Logistics timeline, customs updates.
11. **Return / Refund** – Open dispute → choose refund or return → upload photos → ship via carrier → refund processed.
12. **Cancellation** – Request before seller confirms; may incur fee.

---

## 5. Shopify (SaaS merchant stores – generic flow)
1. **Home / Landing** – Store branding, hero banner, search.
2. **Collection / Search** – Filters, pagination.
3. **Product Detail** – Media carousel, variants, “Add to Cart".
4. **Cart** – Update quantities, estimate shipping.
5. **Checkout** – Guest checkout possible; email capture.
6. **Customer Info** – Email, shipping address.
7. **Shipping Method** – Choose carrier, rates.
8. **Payment** – Shopify Payments, PayPal, Stripe, Apple Pay.
9. **Review Order** – Final review.
10. **Order Confirmation** – Thank‑you page, order #, email receipt.
11. **Order Status Page** – Track URL sent via email.
12. **Returns** – Store‑specific return portal → request → label → ship.
13. **Cancellation** – Cancel from order page before fulfillment; admin flags order as cancelled.

---

## 6. Magento (Open‑source enterprise)
1. **Home** – Banners, promotions, search.
2. **Category / Catalog** – Layered navigation, sorting.
3. **Product Page** – Configurable options, “Add to Cart”.
4. **Mini‑Cart** – Quick view, “View and Edit Cart”.
5. **Cart Page** – Apply coupon, estimate shipping.
6. **Checkout** – Two‑step: Shipping Information → Payment Information.
7. **Shipping** – Select method, address validation.
8. **Payment** – Integrated gateways (Braintree, Authorize.net).
9. **Review & Place Order** – Summary, terms.
10. **Confirmation** – Order success page, email.
11. **Order Management** – Customer portal for tracking.
12. **Return / RMA** – Request Return Merchandise Authorization → admin approves → label.
13. **Cancel Order** – Customer can cancel while status = “Pending”.

---

## 7. WooCommerce (WordPress plugin)
1. **Home** – Theme‑driven storefront, search.
2. **Shop / Category** – Filters via widgets.
3. **Product Detail** – Variable products, “Add to Cart”.
4. **Cart** – Review items, coupon entry.
5. **Checkout** – Single‑page or multi‑step, guest checkout.
6. **Billing / Shipping** – Address fields, optional account creation.
7. **Payment** – PayPal, Stripe, direct bank transfer.
8. **Order Review** – Confirm order.
9. **Thank‑You Page** – Order #, download links (if digital).
10. **My Account** – Order history, “View” → “Track”.
11. **Return** – Plugin‑based return portal (e.g., WooCommerce Returns & Exchanges).
12. **Cancellation** – Cancel from “My Account” before processing.

---

## 8. BigCommerce (SaaS)
1. **Home** – Hero carousel, search bar.
2. **Category / Search** – Faceted navigation.
3. **Product Page** – Media, “Add to Cart”.
4. **Cart** – Edit qty, apply discount.
5. **Checkout** – Guest or login.
6. **Shipping** – Address, carrier selection.
7. **Payment** – BigCommerce Payments, PayPal, Amazon Pay.
8. **Order Review** – Final confirmation.
9. **Confirmation** – Thank‑you page, order details.
10. **Order Status** – Email link to status page.
11. **Returns** – Self‑service returns portal → print label → drop‑off.
12. **Cancellation** – Cancel from order page before fulfillment.

---

## 9. Saleor (Headless, GraphQL‑based)
1. **Landing (Custom Front‑end)** – React/Next.js home page.
2. **Category / Search** – API‑driven product list.
3. **Product Detail** – GraphQL query, “Add to Cart".
4. **Cart** – UI reads cart via API, edit items.
5. **Checkout Flow** – Multi‑step: Email → Shipping → Payment.
6. **Shipping** – Address validation, carrier rates from API.
7. **Payment** – Stripe integration, tokenized card.
8. **Review** – Order summary.
9. **Confirmation** – Front‑end shows order ID, sends email.
10. **Order Tracking** – Customer dashboard fetches order status via API.
11. **Return** – Return request endpoint → admin approves → generate return label.
12. **Cancellation** – Customer can cancel via dashboard while order status = “Pending”.

---

### How to Use This Mapping
- **Compare** each step with your own planned flows to spot gaps or opportunities.
- **Identify** which platforms provide built‑in features you may want to adopt (e.g., Amazon’s “Buy‑Again”, Shopify’s “Order Status Page”).
- **Prioritize** MVP features: Home, Search, Product Detail, Cart, Checkout, Confirmation, and basic Order Tracking.
- **Plan** advanced post‑purchase journeys (returns, cancellations) as separate micro‑services or UI modules.
